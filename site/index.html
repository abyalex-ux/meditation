A calm place to begin

Make a little room for yourself.

Meditation is the practice of noticing the present moment with curiosity and kindness. You do not need to stop your thoughts—just notice them and gently return to your breath.

Mindful presence

Notice your breath, body, thoughts, and feelings without judging them.

Self-compassion

Meet difficult moments with the same patience you would offer a friend.

Connection and meaning

Use meditation to nurture belonging, gratitude, values, and purpose.

Try two quiet minutes today: sit comfortably, feel one full breath, and begin again whenever your attention wanders.
